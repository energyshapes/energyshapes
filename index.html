Our approach

Clear information.
Thoughtful decisions.

Home upgrades can involve significant spending and technical trade-offs. Our aim is to make the research easier to understand, so you can have better conversations with installers and make choices that suit your home.

  • Explain the factors, not just a headline price.Costs vary by location, property, equipment and the work required.
  • Make assumptions visible.Calculators should show what goes into an estimate and what it leaves out.
  • Encourage informed professional advice.Use independent assessments and qualified local contractors for property-specific decisions.
